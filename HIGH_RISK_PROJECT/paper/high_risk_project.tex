Abstract

The Emergency Department or ED is one of the most critical departments of a hospital[]. It is often the first interaction with medical professionals a patient has with serious conditions[] and greatly influences their perception of a hospital and it's services. Since this department is open to any patient 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, the flow of patients through the services of the department is critical for effective treatment and operational stability. One widely recognized way at improving patient flow and feedback is by introducing a Fast Track section within the ED.
